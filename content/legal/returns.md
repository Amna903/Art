We want you to be confident in every acquisition. This policy explains when a work can be returned and how refunds work. It forms part of our **[Terms of Service & Sale](/terms)**.

## 1. Our satisfaction window

For works purchased and shipped by NUA-ARTE, you may request a return within **[14 days]** of delivery, provided the work is **unaltered and in its original condition**, including the Certificate of Authenticity and original crating/packaging.

To start a return, email **[hello@nua-arte.com]** with your name, invoice/reference number, and reason. **Do not ship a work back without written return authorization from us** — we arrange insured transport for fine art.

## 2. Condition requirements

- The work must be returned in the same condition in which it was received.
- It must be repacked in the **original custom crate** and materials.
- Returns that arrive damaged due to improper repacking by the buyer may be refused or subject to a deduction.

## 4. Refunds

- Once we receive and inspect the returned work, we will notify you and issue any refund due within **[10 business days]** to your original payment method.
- Refunds are for the **artwork price**; shipping, insurance, crating, duties, and taxes are refundable only as stated in Sections 3 and 6.

## 5. Damaged or not-as-described works

If a work arrives **damaged**, or materially **differs from its description**:
- Contact us within **[48 hours]** of delivery with photographs of the work and packaging.
- We will arrange return or replacement at **our cost** and issue a **full refund** (including shipping) if a suitable resolution is not available.
- Keep all packaging until the claim is resolved (needed for the shipping insurance claim).
