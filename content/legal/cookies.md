This Cookie Policy explains how NUA-ARTE uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser **local storage**) on **www.nua-arte.com**. It should be read with our **[Privacy Policy](/privacy)**.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. "Local storage" is a related technology that saves data in your browser. We use both to make the Site work and to remember your preferences.
