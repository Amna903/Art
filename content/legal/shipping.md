NUA-ARTE arranges professional, insured shipping for every work, worldwide. Because each piece is an original, shipping is handled individually rather than as standard parcel delivery.

## 1. Where we ship

We ship within the **United States** and internationally, including from our source locations in **[Luanda, Angola / Kinshasa, DRC]** to collectors in the US and elsewhere. Some destinations may be restricted by law, sanctions (OFAC), or carrier limitations; we will tell you if your destination is affected.

## 2. Packing and handling

- Works are packed in **custom-built wooden crates** with museum-grade materials.
- Handling/crating is prepared after your order is confirmed; typical preparation time is **[5–15 business days]**, depending on the work and origin.

## 3. Transit times

- **United States:** approximately **[X–Y business days]** after dispatch.
- **International:** approximately **[X–Y business days]**, plus customs clearance.
- These are estimates. Customs, weather, and carrier delays can extend them. We will share tracking once the work ships.

## 4. Costs

- Shipping, crating, and insurance are **quoted per work and destination** and confirmed on your invoice before you commit.
- Unless stated otherwise, the price of a work **excludes** shipping, insurance, crating, taxes, and import duties.

## 5. Insurance

All shipments are **fully insured** for the purchase value during transit. In case of transit damage, follow the steps in our **[Returns & Refunds Policy](/returns)** (report within [48 hours], keep all packaging).

## 6. Customs, duties, and taxes (international / US import)

- Original paintings, drawings, and sculptures generally qualify for **duty-free** entry into the US under **HTSUS Chapter 97**, but classification is confirmed per work and tariffs/surcharges can change.
- The **importer of record** (usually the buyer, unless agreed otherwise) is responsible for import duties, taxes, and customs fees in the destination country.
- We provide the documentation required for import (artist, medium, value, provenance). Formal US customs entry and a customs bond are generally required for works valued over **[$2,500]**.

## 7. Title and risk

Title passes on full payment (see **[Terms, Section 6](/terms)**). Risk of loss passes on **[delivery to you / handover to the carrier]** — insurance covers the work until delivery.

## 8. Delivery and inspection

- Someone **18+** should be available to receive and sign for the crate.
- Inspect the work on delivery and report any damage within **[48 hours]** with photographs.
- White-glove delivery and installation may be available on request for an additional fee.

## 9. Local collection

Collection in person or private viewings in **[New York / Luanda]** may be arranged by appointment — contact us.
