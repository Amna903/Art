## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Account data:** name/display name, email address, and password (when you create an account or sign in with Google).
- **Inquiries:** your name, email, phone/WhatsApp (optional), and message when you use "Request Price" or "Inquire About a Piece".
- **Newsletter:** your email address when you join the newsletter ("Join the Circle").
- **Reviews:** any content and rating you post on an artwork.
- **Artist applications:** if you register as an Artist, the portfolio and profile details you submit.

**Information collected automatically**
- Device and usage data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URL) and similar data collected through cookies and local storage. See our **[Cookie Policy](/cookies)**.
- Saved artworks are stored in your browser's local storage (and in your account if you are signed in).

**Information from third parties**
- If you sign in with **Google**, we receive your name and email from Google per your Google account settings.

We do **not** intentionally collect special-category data (e.g., health, biometric) and ask that you not submit it.

- To respond to pricing and acquisition inquiries and provide our services.
- To create and manage your account and saved collection.
- To send the newsletter and viewing invitations (where you opted in).
- To publish and moderate reviews.
- To operate, secure, and improve the Site.
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our **[Terms](/terms)**.

**Legal bases (where GDPR applies):** performance of a contract, your consent (newsletter, cookies), our legitimate interests (security, improving the Site), and legal obligations.
